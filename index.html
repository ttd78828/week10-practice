Farmers Market Returns to Wrenfield Square This Saturday

After a two-month winter break, the Wrenfield Farmers Market opens again this Saturday at 8am in Wrenfield Square, with produce, baked goods, and crafts from more than twenty local vendors.

This season adds new hot food row along the square's eat side, so visitors can grab breakfast while they shop instead of waiting until they get home. The market runs every Saturday through October, rain or shine.
